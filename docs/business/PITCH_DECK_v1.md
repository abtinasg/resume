## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Headline:** AI is flooding hiring. Trust is getting harder.

- Job seekers apply a lot → hear almost nothing back (and rarely get actionable feedback)
- AI tools often "help" by inventing details (fake metrics, tools, titles) — risky and credibility-killing
- In the Netherlands, format + language nuance + local expectations add extra friction

**Bottom line:** People don't just need better writing — they need credible positioning and clear likely reasons for rejection.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

**Headline:** careerQ turns your evidence into a clear hiring plan

- **Understand:** Extract verified evidence (projects, skills, impact) and score resume quality
- **Decide:** Match jobs with a Fit Score + gaps + quick wins
- **Improve:** Generate a job-specific tailored resume using only your evidence (traceable lines)

**Note (small):** Think of it as a career partner — it guides decisions and content, but it does not auto-apply to jobs.

---

## Slide 4 — Trust Architecture

**Headline:** Trust-by-design: evidence-anchored output

- **Evidence Extraction:** Use only what exists in your resume/profile
- **Validation Layer:** Catch and flag risky claims (metrics, tools, titles, companies) before you send
- **Evidence Links:** Every suggestion points back to its source evidence
- **Confirmation Loop (small):** You confirm sensitive claims before final output

**Result:** Credible, traceable content that's safer to submit.
